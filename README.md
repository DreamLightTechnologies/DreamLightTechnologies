About Us

Dream Light Technologies is a software development company focused on building modern, scalable, and reliable digital products for startups, businesses, and entrepreneurs.

We transform ideas into practical digital solutions through thoughtful design, robust engineering, intelligent automation, and end-to-end development.

From UI/UX design and full-stack engineering to mobile applications, AI-powered systems, computer vision, deployment, and ongoing support, we help turn business requirements into working products.

What We Focus On

- Modern, responsive, and accessible user interfaces
- Scalable frontend and backend architecture
- Cross-platform mobile and desktop applications
- AI, machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision
- Database design, cloud services, and API integrations
- Maintainable software, testing, deployment, and support

</p>Exploring quantum computing techniques and optimization methods for complex computational problems.

Areas of interest and development:

- Quantum Optimization: Exploring approaches to combinatorial and constrained optimization problems.
- QAOA: Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm for suitable discrete optimization problems.
- VQE: Variational Quantum Eigensolver for applicable quantum chemistry and optimization tasks.
- Hybrid Quantum-Classical Computing: Combining classical optimization algorithms with quantum circuits.
- Optimization Applications: Scheduling, resource allocation, routing, portfolio optimization, and related problem domains.
- Quantum Algorithm Simulation: Prototyping and evaluating quantum algorithms using simulators and available quantum computing frameworks.

</p>Relevant techniques and workflows include:

- NDVI: Normalized Difference Vegetation Index for vegetation assessment using suitable multispectral imagery.
- IoU: Intersection over Union, a metric for evaluating overlap between predicted and ground-truth regions.
- XGBoost: Gradient-boosted decision trees for classification and regression.
- Geospatial analytics: Processing, analysis, and visualization of spatial datasets.
- Remote sensing: Satellite or aerial image analysis and land-cover workflows.
- Custom model integration: Combining computer vision, geospatial data, and machine learning where appropriate.
